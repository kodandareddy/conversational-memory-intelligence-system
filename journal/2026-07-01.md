## Work completed
Instead of rewriting anything, walked through two of the six simple approaches
out loud to check the reasoning actually holds, not just the writing:

1. **Approach 2 (send the whole conversation back) vs. Approach 5 (separate
   notes file).** Confirmed these look different on the surface but fail the
   same way underneath: both require reading an ever-growing amount of text in
   full, so both eventually get slow and expensive. Approach 5 didn't fix the
   problem, it relocated it into a different file.
2. **Approach 6 (search for facts that sound related).** Worked through why
   "sounds related" is not the same as "is the current answer" — search
   matches on topic ("food") but has no sense of time or that two facts are
   competing answers to the same question ("what do I like to eat"). Landed on
   the precise gap: the system has no built-in notion of "still current" vs.
   "outdated"; every saved fact looks equally true forever unless something
   explicitly says otherwise.
